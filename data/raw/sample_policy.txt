Auto Insurance Policy

Coverage:
This insurance policy covers collision damage, theft, fire damage, and weather-related incidents depending on selected coverage options.

Water Damage:
Water damage is covered only if caused by a covered peril. Flood-related damage requires separate flood insurance coverage.

Exclusions:
Fraudulent claims, intentional damage, and unreported vehicle modifications are excluded from coverage.

Claims:
All submitted claims require policy verification, deductible review, and supporting evidence documentation.
